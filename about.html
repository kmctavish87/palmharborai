About

A custom AI build partner for practical operators.

palmharborai.com exists for teams that have real workflows, messy data, and specific decisions to support, not a desire to buy another generic AI subscription.

Philosophy

AI is most valuable when it becomes part of the operating system.

The work is not about novelty. It is about building tools that make a process faster, clearer, more consistent, and easier to manage.

How I work

I combine product thinking, systems design, automation, and AI implementation to turn ambiguous operational problems into usable software. That usually means starting with a workflow, not a model.

The build process is intentionally practical: map the job to be done, test with real examples, create a usable interface, then add the controls needed for adoption.

What matters

Fit The system should match the way your team actually makes decisions.
Control AI should support review, traceability, and clear ownership.
Use A working tool is only successful if people trust it enough to use it.

What I Bring

Strategy, product judgment, and hands-on implementation.

Workflow-first thinking

I start with the process, users, edge cases, and operational constraints.

AI implementation

I design the AI layer around retrieval, extraction, classification, reasoning, and generation where useful.

Application design

I care about the interface, review path, permissions, and day-to-day usability.

Systems integration

I connect data sources, documents, APIs, and tools so the AI does not sit alone.

Operational guardrails

I build for source context, logs, human approval, and clear failure states.

Iteration after launch

I expect the system to improve as real users expose what matters most.
